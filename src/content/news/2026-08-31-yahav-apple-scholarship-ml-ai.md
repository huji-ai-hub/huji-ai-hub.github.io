Yahav Bechavod, a PhD student at the Hebrew University of Jerusalem's School of Engineering and Computer Science, has been selected as a recipient of Apple's prestigious Scholars in AI/ML fellowship for 2020. He is the only Israeli doctoral student to receive the award this year.

The fellowship, awarded for the first time this year, recognizes outstanding PhD students working in the fields of machine learning and artificial intelligence. Recipients are considered leading researchers in these disciplines.

The award highlights the strength of AI and machine learning research at HUJI's School of Engineering and Computer Science.
