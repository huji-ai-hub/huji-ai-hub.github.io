The Edmond and Lily Safra Center for Brain Sciences (ELSC) at the Hebrew University of Jerusalem is launching a research collaboration with the Kempner Institute at Harvard University. The partnership brings together leading researchers in neuroscience, machine learning, cognitive science, and artificial intelligence to study one of science's biggest questions: how intelligence works across both biological and artificial systems.

The collaboration positions the emerging field of NeuroAI, the intersection of brain science and AI research, on HUJI's cross-institutional research map.

Originally announced via Yissum, HUJI's technology transfer company.
