The Kempner Institute for the Study of Natural and Artificial Intelligence at Harvard University and the Edmond and Lily Safra Center for Brain Sciences (ELSC) at the Hebrew University of Jerusalem have announced a new research collaboration in the field of NeuroAI.

The partnership will bring together researchers in neuroscience, machine learning, cognitive science, and artificial intelligence to investigate how intelligence emerges and operates across both biological and artificial systems.

The collaboration reflects a growing research agenda that treats the brain and AI models as mutually informative subjects of study, with findings in one domain expected to shed light on the other.
