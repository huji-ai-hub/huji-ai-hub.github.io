Yahav Bechavod, a doctoral student at the Hebrew University of Jerusalem's School of Engineering and Computer Science, has been named the sole Israeli recipient of Apple's prestigious doctoral scholarship for 2020.

The Apple scholarship was awarded for the first time this year, recognizing outstanding PhD students in the fields of machine learning and artificial intelligence. Bechavod was selected among leading doctoral researchers internationally.

The award highlights the strength of AI and machine learning research at HUJI's School of Engineering and Computer Science.
