A research team led by Prof. Yossi Tam of the Hebrew University School of Pharmacy found that two non-psychoactive cannabis compounds, CBD and CBG, can significantly reduce fat accumulation in the liver and improve metabolic health.

The study, published in the *British Journal of Pharmacology*, shows that the compounds work by creating an alternative energy reserve in the liver and restoring the activity of cellular "cleaning" systems that break down harmful waste.

The findings point to a new plant-based approach for treating chronic liver disease, a condition affecting roughly one third of the adult population in Israel (ages 18 and above) and closely linked to obesity, high blood pressure, and insulin resistance.
