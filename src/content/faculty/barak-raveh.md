Living cells, tissues, and entire organisms are dynamic systems of interacting parts. From the interactions among these parts emerges the collective behavior of these systems, which ultimately yields Life. Our broad aim is to bridge the gap between the large amounts of available data on dynamic biological systems and our limited quantitative understanding of their behavior.
